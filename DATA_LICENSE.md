# Data terms

The code is under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The data in `data/`, and the files
`make data` downloads, are not: they keep the terms of their providers.

## MERGE (CPTEC/INPE)

The CPTEC MERGE product data in this repository are for restricted use and may not be sold. Use of the data is subject to the following conditions:

- Commercial use of the CPTEC MERGE product data is not permitted.
- The data may not be resold or redistributed commercially.
- For more details about the data and its restrictions, see the [MERGE README](https://ftp.cptec.inpe.br/modelos/tempo/MERGE/GPM/MERGE_READ-ME.pdf).

For any questions about data use, please contact CPTEC/INPE at atende.cptec@inpe.br.

Reference: Rozante, J. R., Moreira, D. S., de Goncalves, L. G. G. and Vila, D. A. (2010).
Combining TRMM and surface observations of precipitation: technique and validation over
South America. *Weather and Forecasting*, 25(3), 885–894. https://doi.org/10.1175/2010WAF2222325.1

## State boundaries (IBGE)

`data/BR_UF_2022/BR_UF_2022.zip` is the 2022 federative-unit boundary layer of the
Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE), *Malha Territorial 2022*
(https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/organizacao-do-territorio/malhas-territoriais/15774-malhas.html),
published as open public data; cite IBGE as the source.

## Natural Earth

Maps made without `--shapefile` draw coastlines and borders from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).
